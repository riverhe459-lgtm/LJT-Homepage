Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. He is pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Science (2024-Present) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. Professor He also previously advised him during his undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU). He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.
