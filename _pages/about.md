About
======
Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.

His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning.

Research interests include:
* LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
* Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
* LLM truthfulness and Interpretability

PhD supervisor: Professor Junxian He, Professor at HKUST NLP Group. Professor Junxian He previously advised Junteng Liu during undergraduate studies at SJTU. HKUST NLP Group is a research group at Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, led by Professor Junxian He, and focuses on natural language processing research.
